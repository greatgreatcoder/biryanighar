Our Delicious Story

Great biryani cannot be hurried. We layer fragrant rice, marinated meats and whole spices, seal the handi with dough, and let time do the rest. Rooted in tradition, plated with restraint.

Sealed, Slow And Served At Your Table

Every handi is sealed with dough and cooked dum, slowly, so the steam carries the saffron and spice through each grain of rice.

Aged Basmati, Hand-Ground Masalas

Aged basmati, hand-ground masalas and patient dum cooking: simple, honest ingredients given the time they deserve.
